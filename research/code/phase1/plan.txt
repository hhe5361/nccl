1. Implement B1

2. Smoke test
- Run B0 and W=4 on a small AllReduce ring simple case.

3. Baseline runs
- Collect B0 first for every workload before sweeping W.
- Keep placement and env fixed.

4. Primary B1 sweep
- Workload A:
  AllReduce / Ring / Simple / 32MB
  - intra-rack: worker01-04
  - cross-rack: worker01-08
  - sweep W=1,2,4,8

5. Secondary sweep
- Workload B:
  AllReduce / Ring / LL128 / 32MB
  - intra-rack and cross-rack
  - sweep W=1,2,4,8

6. Large message sweep
- Repeat A and B with 256MB.

7. Optional aggressive workload
- AllToAll / Simple / 32MB and 256MB
- Prefer cross-rack first.
- Sweep W=1,2,4,8.

8. Data extraction
- Parse raw logs into:
  - run_summary.csv
  - event_timeseries.csv
  - occupancy_summary.csv
  - stall_summary.csv

9. Figures
- F1: W vs throughput
- F2: W vs peak send burst
- F3: W vs max/p99 receiver occupancy
- F4: timeline comparison for W=1 vs W=8
- F5: Pareto plot (peak burst vs throughput)

10. Interpretation
- Expect W=1 to minimize burst but hurt throughput.
- Expect W=8 to maximize throughput but increase burst and occupancy.
- Goal is to show best fixed W depends on workload.
- Use this result to justify B2/B3 adaptive policy.
